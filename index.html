I am a PhD student at HKUST, advised by Prof. Charles Zhang. My research lies at the intersection of program analysis, formal reasoning, and reliable foundation-model agents. I am interested in how explicit specifications, structured representations, and constraint solving can complement LLMs and VLMs.

My work explores two complementary questions: how to make high-precision analysis efficient for interactive use, and how to structure agent tasks so that decisions can be derived and checked. SPONGE addresses the first through online value-flow queries. My recent work, ReCoNav, explores the second in constrained long-horizon vision-and-language navigation, using instruction contracts and symbolic reasoning alongside model-based grounding.

I am interested in research internships and collaborations on reliable AI agents and the interface between AI and formal analysis. Please feel free to get in touch.

ReCoNav: Reliable Constrained Long-Horizon Vision-and-Language Navigation via a Determination Engine

Manuscript under review, 2026.

An instruction contract formalizes scoped navigation requirements over complete routes. A determination engine separates uncertain semantic grounding from decisions that can be derived and checked, connecting target selection with constraint-aware planning.
