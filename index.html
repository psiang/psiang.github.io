I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at HKUST, advised by Prof. Charles Zhang.

My research focuses on reliable reasoning and planning for embodied agents. I study how agents can turn natural-language instructions into executable plans while respecting constraints over long tasks.

My background is in static program analysis. Across these areas, I am interested in combining learned judgments with explicit structure, logical reasoning, and verification.

Research interests

  • Reliable embodied agents

    Grounding instructions, reasoning about constraints, and checking decisions before execution.

  • Vision-and-language navigation

    Long-horizon navigation with requirements that shape both goal selection and the route.

  • Program analysis

    Precise and efficient value-flow reasoning for interactive software analysis.

Selected research & publications

UNDER REVIEW2026 · Embodied AI

ReCoNav: Reliable Constrained Long-Horizon Vision-and-Language Navigation via a Determination Engine

ReCoNav combines an instruction contract with a determination engine to propagate constraints across interpretation, grounding, and route planning. It uses derived evidence to determine choices, verify judgments, and guide repair, while vision-language models resolve the remaining semantics.

SPONGE: Adaptive Boundary-Anchored Indexing for Online Value-Flow Queries

Sixiang Peng, Chenyang Sun, Wei Chen, Bowen Zhang, Charles Zhang

SPONGE accelerates online value-flow queries by selectively indexing feasible path segments at procedure boundaries, then reusing them during search.
